INTERPRETATION

Confidence-targeted feedback produced a statistically significant
but small improvement in F1 compared with random feedback selection
across the 20 paired runs.

Most of the improvement occurred during the early feedback cycles,
with the highest mean F1 of 98.29% occurring at Cycle 2. Performance
then plateaued, indicating diminishing returns.

LIMITATIONS

The starting F1 was already high, leaving limited room for improvement.

Reference labels were generated using frozen heuristic rules rather
than independent production ground truth.

Administrator feedback was simulated using the frozen reference
labels rather than being supplied by real Azure administrators.

Therefore, this experiment evaluates the behaviour of the proposed
feedback mechanism under controlled conditions and should not be
interpreted as production validation.
